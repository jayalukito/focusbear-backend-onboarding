## Reflection

**1. How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?**
*   They create predictable, dedicated spaces for different types of communication. Instead of interrupting deep coding work with random status checks, developers know exactly when and where to share updates (Stand-ups), when to discuss requirements (Refinement), and when to critique the process (Retrospectives). 

**2. Which ceremony do you think is most important for your role, and why?**
*   **The Daily Stand-up.** Because I work a split schedule (Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays), clear and concise stand-ups are critical. If I am blocked on a C# backend task on Tuesday afternoon, raising it immediately ensures the team can help unblock me or take over the ticket while I am away on Wednesday, rather than leaving the work stalled.
