# Teamwork & Professionalism Reflection
**Role:** Developer Intern

## Reflection

**1. Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?**
*   Yes, while working as a Front-End Software Engineer in IT consulting, I saw how effective daily stand-ups and a blameless culture during code reviews kept the team aligned and professional without feeling micromanaged. 

**2. What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?**
*   Avoid unnecessary technical jargon when speaking with non-technical stakeholders.
*   Assume positive intent in all written communication.
*   Use accessible language and provide quick written summaries after verbal discussions to ensure everyone is on the same page.

**3. How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?**
*   Celebrate small team wins and successful pull request merges.
*   Share interesting technical discoveries or helpful new tools with the team.
*   Be approachable and willing to pair-program or help unblock a colleague.

**4. How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?**
*   Review the agenda and any relevant project tickets beforehand.
*   Draft specific questions or blockers I need to discuss.
*   Ensure my local development environment is running and ready in case I need to screen-share my code.

**5. What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?**
*   Anticipating edge cases and writing tests for them before QA flags an issue.
*   Reviewing the backlog and voluntarily picking up small bugs or technical debt when my main tasks are blocked.

**6. How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?**
*   **Follow-up:** Send a polite, concise nudge via chat after 24 hours (e.g., "Hi, just bubbling this up—let me know if you need any more context from me!").
*   **Escalation:** If I am completely blocked for 48 hours with no response, I would bring it up in the daily stand-up or escalate to my direct manager, clearly outlining the blocker and its impact on the sprint.

**7. What are some things you won't do?**
*   I won't merge code without an approved code review.
*   I won't bypass failing CI/CD pipeline checks.
*   I won't engage in office gossip or unprofessional behavior.
