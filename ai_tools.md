# AI Tools Experience
**Role:** Developer Intern

## 1. Which AI tools did you try?
I used both **Gemini** and **ChatGPT**.

## 2. What worked well? What didn’t?
*   **What worked well:** Using the tools to analyze specific code snippets to find bugs was highly effective. I also noticed that providing clear, detailed context beforehand significantly improved the accuracy of the responses.
*   **What didn't work well:** Conversely, expecting perfect answers without supplying that necessary upfront context generally led to less useful or incorrect suggestions.

## 3. When do you think AI is most useful for coding?
AI is often best utilized for **debugging** tricky issues and quickly **generating boilerplate code**. It dramatically speeds up the initial setup of new components and acts as a highly capable assistant when troubleshooting specific errors.
