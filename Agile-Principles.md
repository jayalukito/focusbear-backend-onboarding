## Reflection

**1. What do you think are the biggest benefits and challenges of Agile?**
*   **Benefits:** High adaptability to change, faster delivery of core features, and a constant feedback loop that ensures the product actually meets user needs.
*   **Challenges:** The lack of rigid structure can sometimes lead to scope creep if not managed properly, and the focus on "working software" can occasionally result in neglected technical documentation.

**2. How can Agile principles help improve your role and responsibilities?**
*   Applying Agile principles will help me break down complex technical tasks—whether I am writing backend endpoints in C#, configuring server environments, or training machine learning models—into smaller, independently testable units. It encourages me to seek early feedback on pull requests rather than waiting until a massive feature is completely finished.

## Task

**1. Summarize the main differences between Scrum and Kanban in your own words.**
*   Scrum is like a time-boxed sprint: you lock in a set amount of work for two weeks and race to finish it. Kanban is like a continuous conveyor belt: you pull in new tasks as soon as you finish the current ones, keeping the flow steady and preventing bottlenecks.

**2. Identify one Agile principle you think will be most useful in your work.**
*   **Principle:** *"Simplicity—the art of maximizing the amount of work not done—is essential."* 
*   **Application:** This will remind me to avoid over-engineering solutions and focus strictly on delivering the most direct, efficient code required to solve the immediate problem at hand.
