# Workspace & Ergonomics Reflection

## Reflection

**1. What equipment changes can you make to improve your workspace setup?**
*   **External Monitor & Stand:** Adding an external monitor and a laptop stand to elevate my main screen to eye level, which is essential for expanding screen real estate during long development sessions.
*   **Peripherals:** Using a separate, ergonomic mechanical keyboard and mouse to prevent wrist strain while coding.

**2. What behavioural changes can you implement to improve posture and reduce strain?**
*   **Seated Posture:** Sitting upright with my feet flat on the floor and my lower back supported.
*   **Active Breaks:** Incorporating active stretching during breaks—specifically shoulder mobility and wrist exercises to maintain my flexibility for badminton and gym sessions, while reducing the stiffness that comes from typing.
*   **Eye Care:** Practicing the 20-20-20 rule to reduce eye fatigue when staring at code for extended periods.

**3. How can you remind yourself to maintain good posture and take breaks throughout the day?**
*   I will configure Focus Bear to trigger mandatory 2-minute movement breaks every 45 minutes, ensuring I step away from the desk rather than working through fatigue.
