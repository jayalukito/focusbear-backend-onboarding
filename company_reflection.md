# Understanding Focus Bear’s Mission & Vision

### Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
To help people struggling with having a long attention span which is commonly found in people diagnosed with ADHD. Focus bear helps to create routines, block distracting apps and websites.  
### What problem is Focus Bear solving?
It helps people with a short attention span by removing distractions, building good habits, eliminating decision fatigue, and using visual cues to increase focus. All of these features help people suffering from short attention span to regain their focus and increase their motivation in somewhat of a gamified habit builder
### Why do you think this mission is important?
I think this mission is really imporntant because it helps younger generation to fight regain their focus, to build good habits, and most importantly feel in control and less distracted. In addition, For those that are suffering ADHD or autism this will help to support them throught their day to day tasks and help them to finish daily assignments while building good habits. In conclusion, It helps all kinds of people, affected or not affected by neurodevelopmental disability or not. 
### How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?
Honestly, I've always struggled with short attention span myself. More often than not I find myself stuck on a phone screen for hours scrolling my hours and time away. In addition, I have always wanted to build meaningful habits such as morning gratitude, journaling, exercise which are all of the things focus bear supports. Because of this I'm highly excited to try this app out and actually give meaningful suggestion and meaningful ideas to implement in the application
### Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
Yes I really do, like I mentioned, I have a really bad attention span. This issue was made worse with newer social media application promoting scroll type content which made it ultimately worse. Focus Bear directly solves this particular issue which made me personally interested to try the applcation and more than happy to be welcomed in the team.
