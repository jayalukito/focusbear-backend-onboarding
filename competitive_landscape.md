# Competitive Landscape Analysis
**Role:** Developer Intern

## Focus Bear vs. Opal
*   **Opal:** Features a very nice UI and effective hard block modes. However, it costs $100 a year, and its VPN approach can drain battery life.
*   **Focus Bear:** Focus Bear is a more comprehensive tool that includes guided morning routines and productivity-boosting breaks. What's more, it is specifically designed for neurodivergent individuals.

## Focus Bear vs. Freedom
*   **Freedom:** Provides excellent cross-device syncing for blocking distractions across platforms.
*   **Focus Bear:** Freedom lacks habit-building features. Focus Bear is convenient because it combines cross-device blocking with real-time habit guidance and scheduled micro-breaks.

## Focus Bear vs. Cold Turkey
*   **Cold Turkey:** Offers nuclear-level blocking that is impossible to bypass on desktop devices. It does not have a mobile version.
*   **Focus Bear:** Focus Bear uses motivation and habit-building rather than the strict force of Cold Turkey. It is also highly convenient since it works across Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android.

## Focus Bear vs. Tiimo
*   **Tiimo:** Helps manage overwhelm by acting as a visual planner to lower the cost of starting tasks.
*   **Focus Bear:** While both support neurodivergent users, Focus Bear actively blocks digital distractions using AI, which Tiimo does not do.

## Focus Bear vs. Routinery
*   **Routinery:** Focuses heavily on sequential timers for building daily habits and routines.
*   **Focus Bear:** Focus Bear goes a step further by integrating routine tracking with actual system-wide distraction blocking. This ensures users complete their routines without getting sidetracked by social media.
