# Reflection
I used Opal personally as a daily distracting app blocker. They are successful in making bad habits hard while making staying off your phone very confinient. 

## What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?
I'd say the habit builder feature is the one that made me interested compared to Opal.
## If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?
To be honest, I'd use it because of the habit builder and its detailed stats board.
## What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
Compared to Opal, Opal has an achievement system which makes using the app addicting and more interesting. In addition, the UI and UX feels more fluid this would be the biggest game changer for me. 
## Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?
I'd say UI and UX feature. They're probably the best improvement that Focus Bear needs. I think the app has a really solid idea and could go far, however the UI and UX really turns a lot people down from using the app. Making the app more intuitive and more alive would be the best step that focus bear can take in my humble opinion.
